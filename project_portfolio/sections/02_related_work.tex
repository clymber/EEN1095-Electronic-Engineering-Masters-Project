\section{Machine-Learning Modelling of Scattering-Parameter Responses}
\label{sec:related-work}

Schierholz \textit{et al.} introduced an open database of PCB interconnects and
demonstrated neural-network prediction of selected S-parameter magnitude and
phase \cite{schierholz2021sipi}. Their results proved the feasibility of data-driven
S-parameter modelling and reproduced important response features. But prediction
accuracy deteriorated for electrically long links with stronger frequency-dependent
behaviour. It highlights a central difficulty for broadband modelling: more complex
responses are harder to represent
accurately.

Broadband prediction also raises an architectural challenge because neighbouring
frequency points are strongly correlated. Swaminathan \textit{et al.} identified
poor scaling of conventional fully connected networks for such responses
\cite{swaminathan2020demystifying}. Torun \textit{et al.} addressed the problem with
the spectral transposed convolutional neural network (S-TCNN), using transposed
convolution to generate a complete spectral response and exploit correlation
along frequency \cite{torun2019spectral}. It provides a more structured
alternative to treating frequency points independently, although the
architecture itself does not guarantee physically valid predictions.

Hence, physical consistency is an additional requirement. Chen and Tan
showed that an unconstrained neural network could produce non-physical
IL predictions, demonstrating that prediction error alone is
insufficient for evaluating an SI model \cite{chen2021physics}. Akinwande
\textit{et al.} extended the principle to broadband complex $S_{11}$ and
$S_{21}$ using a complex-valued neural network with passivity and causality
enforcement \cite{akinwande2024complex}. This research preserves phase information and
addresses physical behaviour explicitly, but still targets selected responses, not the
complete multi-port matrix. Complex multi-port surrogates have been demonstrated for
other radio-frequency structures \cite{karahan2024multiport}. However, the PCB SI
studies reviewed here focus on selected responses, instead of learned,
design-conditioned prediction of every complex entry of a broadband 12-port interconnect
matrix. It motivates the reciprocal full 12-port formulation evaluated in this work.
